# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer or User Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers help the team understand customer needs, reduce friction in the user journey, and validate that the solution is usable, accessible, and aligned to the product goal.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing
- Turn user needs and business goals into flows, wireframes, and prototypes
- Define accessibility and interaction standards for the product experience
- Collaborate with Product Managers on problem framing and prioritization
- Partner with Developers and QA to validate experience quality and acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework caused by unclear requirements or poor experience design
- Support evidence-based product decisions

### Typical Communication
- Discovery sessions and research synthesis
- Design reviews and prototype walkthroughs
- UX acceptance criteria and usability feedback during delivery

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to validate problem statements and prioritize user pain points
- Collaborates with Developers on feasibility, flow design, and trade-offs
- Partners with QA/Testing on usability checks, accessibility validation, and acceptance evidence
- Keeps the Project Manager informed of design-related dependencies or timeline risks

---

## Tech Lead / Software Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Software Architects guide technical design, standards, and implementation quality. They ensure the team can deliver complex work safely while balancing product needs, technical debt, and delivery risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, architecture patterns, and standards
- Help break down complex work into coherent, manageable delivery units
- Review design trade-offs, risks, and technical dependencies
- Support Developers with technical guidance and design decisions
- Coordinate with Project Managers and Product Managers on delivery constraints, sequencing, and risks

### Goals
- Maintain a sustainable, scalable, and maintainable technical approach
- Reduce avoidable delivery risk and architectural drift
- Support predictable execution across the team

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and design discussions
- Technical risk updates during planning and delivery standups
- Cross-team dependency conversations with Platform, Security, or Integrations partners

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to translate roadmap goals into feasible technical approaches
- Supports Developers by clarifying design intent, standards, and implementation trade-offs
- Advises Project Managers on technical dependencies, sequencing, and risk mitigation
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on testability, automation opportunities, and release risk

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs, operational context, and business requirements into clear documentation and shared understanding across the project team.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business problems, requirements, and process flows
- Document acceptance criteria, edge cases, and decision points
- Support requirements prioritization and scope trade-offs with Product Managers
- Create alignment between stakeholders and the delivery team
- Track process and workflow impacts across functions or systems

### Goals
- Improve requirement clarity and reduce ambiguity in delivery
- Align business expectations with execution realities
- Help teams make better decisions with less churn

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirement workshops
- Requirements reviews and clarification threads
- Acceptance criteria sign-off and change-impact discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to refine problem statements, backlog items, and business value assumptions
- Collaborates with Developers and QA/Testing to ensure requirements are specific, testable, and actionable
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing dependencies, process impacts, and stakeholder concerns
- Helps Stakeholders understand how changes affect workflows, users, and operations

---

## DevOps / Site Reliability / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps, SRE, and Release Engineers support operational readiness, environment reliability, automation, and safe deployment. They help teams deliver software with confidence while minimizing production risk.

### Responsibilities
- Manage CI/CD pipelines, environment setup, and release automation
- Support monitoring, logging, alerting, and incident readiness
- Help define deployment windows, rollback plans, and service health checks
- Support reliability, scalability, and operational guardrails for delivery teams
- Partner with Developers and QA to validate release readiness and post-deploy health

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and improve release confidence
- Increase service reliability and operational visibility
- Support faster, safer delivery with fewer manual bottlenecks

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment review meetings
- Post-deploy validation and incident triage discussions
- Platform and observability health check updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to support build, test, and deploy automation
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on smoke tests, staging validation, and release sign-off
- Partners with Project Managers on release timing, dependency management, and communication plans
- Supports Product Managers and Stakeholders by confirming operational readiness and rollback preparedness

---

## Security / Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads ensure the project meets required controls, user-data protections, and risk expectations. They help teams identify and mitigate concerns early before they become blockers or incidents.

### Responsibilities
- Review product and technical decisions for security and privacy implications
- Define control requirements, review processes, and compliance considerations
- Support risk assessments and incident preparedness
- Partner with stakeholders on data handling, access, and trust requirements
- Coordinate remediation and escalation when security or privacy concerns arise

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risk throughout the lifecycle
- Build trust with users, stakeholders, and regulators
- Prevent late-stage blockers and incident escalations

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and architecture discussions
- Risk escalation and remediation coordination
- Privacy impact or compliance review updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to ensure user and business requirements are consistent with security and privacy constraints
- Advises Developers and Tech Leads on secure design patterns and required controls
- Partners with QA/Testing on verification of security and privacy requirements
- Coordinates with Project Managers on risk tracking, escalations, and stakeholder communication

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The role set should be adapted to project size: some responsibilities may be shared across roles, while larger initiatives may require dedicated specialists.
